Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor V2.0

Analog output connected to ESP32 GPIO35 (ADC1), powered from 3.3 V. The dashboard shows measured millivolts until dry/wet endpoints are recorded. Lower voltage generally indicates wetter conditions. A calibrated relative percentage is not laboratory volumetric water content.

Keep the connector and electronic components above the soil and water line. Supplier information: Nyereka; the supplied product listing is not an independent calibration certificate.

Gateway connection

Readings arrive through the local Python receiver and cloud bridge. Keep both running on your computer. If either stops, this dashboard marks the last reading stale.

Pump ON/OFF status is reported by the ESP32. SIM800C sends crop and pump messages after its recipient number and wiring are configured. Your computer stores received readings in SQLite; the dashboard keeps the latest 240 uploaded readings.
